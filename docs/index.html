About

War Arts Productions is an independent record label from Portugal founded in 2006, dedicated exclusively to the darkest and most uncompromising corners of extreme metal — black metal, death metal, and everything that bleeds between them. For nearly two decades the label has released and distributed cassettes, CDs, LPs and splits from both underground cult acts and internationally recognized names such as Marduk, Immortal, Taake, Revenge, Mysticum, Blasphemy, Black Witchery, Diocletian and Xasthur, alongside a strong roster of Portuguese extreme metal bands including Velório, Morte Incandescente, Irae, Baal and Ruach Raah.

War Arts Productions has operated as a sublabel of Electric Mass Records since 2022, continuing its mission of putting out physical, no-compromise editions for collectors and devotees of extreme music worldwide.

  • Established: 2006 (Portugal)
  • Focus: Extreme metal (black/death/war metal)
  • Formats: Cassette, CD, LP, EP
  • Affiliation: Electric Mass Records (sublabel since 2022)
